The A3A Ultimate addon extends the base Antistasi experience with advanced features including zombie waves, anomaly fields, vehicle lockpicking, black market systems, and comprehensive zone management. It's designed to be modular and configurable through CBA settings.

🎯 Key Features

  • 🧟 Zombie Wave System: Complete zombie spawning, crater effects, and multi-wave attacks
  • 🗺️ Zone Management: Advanced zone revelation with distance-based and random selection
  • 🚗 Vehicle Lockpicking: Comprehensive vehicle locking/unlocking with engineer requirements
  • 🌀 STALKER Integration: Anomaly fields and emission storms with Diwako Anomalies and TTS Emission compatibility
  • 💰 Black Market: Dynamic vehicle pricing with zone-based discounts and requirement checking
  • 🎯 Ammunition Management: Forbidden items system and loot array processing
  • ⚙️ CBA Settings: Extensive configuration options for all systems
